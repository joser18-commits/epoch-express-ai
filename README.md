# History Weaver AI

Build a private AI-powered web application called History Studio AI for a single user (me). Do not build authentication, user accounts, subscriptions, or payment features. The goal is to quickly create high-quality history videos for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and YouTube.



The app should let me enter any historical topic or question. The AI should research reliable historical sources, create an accurate script (30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes), clearly distinguish documented history from dramatized dialogue, generate consistent scenes in the selected art style, create AI narration with multiple voice options, add cinematic music and sound effects, generate subtitles automatically, and export ready-to-upload videos in vertical (9:16) and horizontal (16:9) formats.



Support multilingual narration and subtitles. Allow me to generate videos in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Russian, and more. Voices should sound natural and allow me to choose different ages and styles, including adult male, adult female, young man, young woman, child, and elderly storyteller. The app should also be able to translate the script while preserving historical accuracy and natural speech.



Include storytelling styles (Documentary, Epic Anime, Dramatic, Emotional, Funny, Kid-Friendly, Movie Trailer, and Podcast), art styles (Anime, Pixar-like, Realistic, Comic Book, Watercolor, 3D, Medieval Painting, and Manga), and a Historical Accuracy slider (Beginner, Student, College, Historian).



Build the app with a clean, fast, mobile-friendly interface focused on content creation. Prioritize a working MVP over advanced features. Use modular architecture so authentication, subscriptions, cloud storage, collaboration, and app store publishing can be added later without redesigning the appl
